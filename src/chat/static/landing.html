Kaya

A home lab of AI agents that work for me

What started as a simple chatbot quickly became much more.

Kaya is what I call the ecosystem I have built at home: a chatbot for my friends, an assistant I talk to from my phone, agents that turn ideas into code and fix their own bugs, and a set of small scouts that tell me when something is worth knowing. Everything runs on my own hardware.

Built by Gustavo Abreu, AI engineer and forward deployed engineer.

How I build

Private by default. My friends' messages, photos and voice notes are processed at home and are never sent to a cloud AI.

Agents propose, a person approves. Anything that writes, merges or deploys waits for my yes.

Measured, not assumed. Changes to the group chat are checked against a test set before they go live; when a fine-tuned model did not beat a stock one, the stock one won.

Como construo

Privado por defeito. As mensagens, fotos e áudios dos meus amigos são processados em casa e nunca vão para uma IA na nuvem.

Os agentes propõem, uma pessoa aprova. Tudo o que escreve, junta ou publica espera pelo meu sim.

Medido, não assumido. As mudanças ao chat do grupo são testadas contra um conjunto de testes antes de irem para produção; quando um modelo afinado não bateu um modelo de base, ganhou o de base.
